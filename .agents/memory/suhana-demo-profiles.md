---
name: Suhana demo profiles
description: Product rule for test profiles used to make discovery explorable.
---

Sample profiles are preview fixtures, not represented as real or live members. Keep them visibly marked and exclude them from paid actions.

**Why:** Preview data helps test discovery while avoiding misleading users or charging credits to interact with fictional accounts.

**How to apply:** Preserve the distinction whenever adding demo/test accounts or credit-gated features.
